## Profile

PhD candidate in evolutionary biology at the University of Bologna, with a visiting period at the Arctic University of Norway. My research centres on **mitonuclear coevolution**, integrating comparative genomics, phylogenomics, and small-RNA biology. Primarily a bioinformatician, I combine genome-scale data analysis with foundational wet-lab skills.
